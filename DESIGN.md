# Theme directions

The law and clinic themes retain their established independent design systems
from template-library. Legal: navy/ivory, classical architectural imagery,
Noto Naskh Arabic and Libre Baskerville; clinic: white/teal, warm reception
photography, practical service/appointment composition. These are adaptations
for Arabic-first independent deployment, not redesigns.

Portfolio: design/portfolio-hero-work.png and portfolio-about-contact.png are
the generated visual references. True white #ffffff, royal indigo #2c36a6,
orange #e98450, pale blue #eff1fa. Cairo Arabic/Bricolage English. Header 96px,
hero two columns 3:1, right-aligned 72–92px heading, compact monogram to left.
Thin section rules, large image-led work panels, open service rows, generous
about section and indigo contact band. No client statistics or endorsements.
The individual work images are fictional concept projects. Forms remain local.

Shared behavior: keyboard-visible focus, working mobile navigation and language
switches, reduced motion, honest success/error states. Page layout and tokens
stay local to each theme. Locale swaps content, fonts, alignment and direction.

## Reference verification

Compared the generated hero/work and about/contact references with actual Chrome
screenshots at 1440×1100 using view_image. Checked header order and labels,
hero text and two-column hierarchy, Cairo weight and size, exact indigo/white
tokens, monogram, work filters and image frame, open services rows, and the
indigo contact treatment. The decorative browser bar in the concept is not
rendered as website UI. The campaign photo is a separately generated matching
asset; its exact poster artwork differs from the concept. Scope adds two
concept cases with the same image-led project structure. No unsupported hero
labels or claims were added. Fixed the desktop menu visibility after correcting
CSS import order and reversed directional icons for Arabic. Tested all three
themes at 1440 and 390 pixels in both languages: no horizontal overflow or
broken images. Forms and filters were exercised through Chrome; IAB was absent.
