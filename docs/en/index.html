Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about appointments, preparation and this demonstration.

How do I request an appointment?
Choose a service and an appointment type, then try the form at the top of this page. It prepares a demonstration request locally; nothing is sent and no real appointment is booked.
What should I bring to an appointment?
A real clinic would explain what to bring when confirming your appointment. This sample page does not provide clinical instructions or personalised medical advice.
Can I choose a family appointment?
Select “Children and family care” in the service field to see how a family-focused enquiry works. A real clinic would confirm the appropriate appointment and any requirements.
Are phone appointments available?
The phone format is included to demonstrate appointment selection. It does not indicate real availability, clinical suitability or an actual service offering.
Can I change or cancel a request?
There is no real booking in this demonstration. You can change the selected service or prepare another sample request. No information is stored after you leave the page.
